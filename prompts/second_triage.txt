TASK — INCLUSION DECISION:

Step 1 — Gene-phenotype link:
Is the gene clearly reported for this phenotype or a phenotypically overlapping condition?
Evidence that satisfies this step:
  - Gene reported in literature or OMIM for this phenotype, OR
  - ClinVar entries for this gene list the patient's disease as an associated condition.
Speculative, pathway-only, or indirect links do NOT satisfy this step.
  YES → continue to Step 2
  NO or speculative → Decision: EXCLUDE

Step 2 — Probable pathogenicity:
Does at least one of the following apply?
  - ClinVar Pathogenic or Likely pathogenic, OR
  - Strong ACMG criterion present (PVS1, PS1, PS3, or equivalent), OR
  - Variant rare (AF < 0.1%) with LoF, splice disruption, or CADD ≥ 20, OR
  - Variant homozygous or hemizygous (including "hom" in X-linked genes) in a known recessive/X-linked gene with low population frequency, OR
  - Variant or position reported as disease-causing in cited literature.
Note: "no functional study" does NOT mean no pathogenicity support. Rarity, high CADD,
homozygosity, and a strong gene-disease link are positive signals even without wet-lab data.
  YES → Decision: INCLUDE
  NO → Decision: EXCLUDE

Exception — Compound heterozygous:
If two or more variants affect the same gene, INCLUDE if Step 1 is clearly established
AND at least one of the variants satisfies Step 2.

Important: missing tool results (failed retrieval, no rsID, no LitVar hits) do NOT
by themselves justify EXCLUDE. Evaluate the signals that ARE present (frequency,
CADD, zygosity, gene-disease association) before deciding.
