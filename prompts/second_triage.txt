TASK — INCLUSION DECISION:

Decide whether this variant should proceed to the final clinical conclusion.
INCLUDE only variants with real clinical significance: a clear gene-phenotype link
AND probable pathogenicity. Both criteria must be supported by evidence.
Variants included here appear in the final clinical report — be selective.

INCLUDE requires evidence for BOTH:

1. GENE-PHENOTYPE LINK — the gene is known or strongly suspected to cause the
   patient's phenotype:
   - Gene reported in literature or OMIM for this phenotype or a phenotypically
     overlapping condition, OR
   - ClinVar entries for this gene list the patient's disease as associated condition.

2. PROBABLE PATHOGENICITY — the variant itself is likely damaging:
   - ClinVar Pathogenic or Likely pathogenic, OR
   - Strong ACMG criterion applies (PVS1, PS1, PS3, or equivalent), OR
   - Variant is rare (AF < 0.1%) with predicted functional impact (LoF, splice
     disruption, or high CADD score ≥ 20), OR
   - Variant is homozygous in a known recessive gene with low population frequency, OR
   - Variant or position reported as disease-causing in literature.

   Note: "no functional study" does NOT equal no pathogenicity support. Rarity,
   high CADD, homozygosity, and strong gene-disease link are all positive signals
   even when no wet-lab data exists for this specific variant.

EXCEPTION — Compound heterozygous candidates:
   If two or more variants are present in the same gene in this patient, INCLUDE
   if criterion 1 is met, even when individual variant pathogenicity evidence is limited.

EXCLUDE if:
   - Gene-phenotype link is absent or only speculative (no literature, no OMIM
     association, no ClinVar disease entries for this phenotype), OR
   - Pathogenicity evidence is truly absent: variant is common (AF > 1%), CADD low,
     no LoF mechanism, ClinVar Benign or Likely benign with no conflicting evidence.

Important: missing tool results (failed retrieval, no rsID, no LitVar hits) do NOT
by themselves justify EXCLUDE. Evaluate the variant-level signals that ARE present
(frequency, CADD, zygosity, gene-disease association) before deciding.

Output exactly three lines and nothing else:
Include-case: <strongest reason to include, ≤15 words>
Exclude-case: <strongest reason to exclude, ≤15 words>
Decision: INCLUDE or EXCLUDE

Example output (strong case — known gene, LoF):

Exclude-case: no variant-level functional data beyond prediction
Decision: INCLUDE

Example output (included — VUS but strong signals):
Include-case: known LCA gene; homozygous VUS, very low AF, high CADD
Exclude-case: ClinVar VUS, no functional study for this specific variant
Decision: INCLUDE

Example output (excluded — missing gene link):
Include-case: variant rare and predicted damaging by CADD
Exclude-case: no gene-disease link for this phenotype; gene unrelated
Decision: EXCLUDE

Example output (excluded — common variant, no signal):

Exclude-case: AF 3%, CADD 8, ClinVar Benign, no LoF mechanism
