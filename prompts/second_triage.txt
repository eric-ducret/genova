TASK — INCLUSION DECISION:

Decide whether this variant should proceed to the final clinical conclusion,
or be set aside because it does not plausibly explain the patient's phenotype.

When the case is balanced or uncertain, INCLUDE. Exclude only when the reasoning
points to a clear, affirmative reason the variant cannot explain the phenotype.

Absence or sparseness of evidence is not sufficient for exclusion. Gene-level and
variant-level evidence should be considered separately. Limited or missing 
variant-level evidence does not justify exclusion when gene-level evidence is relevant.

INCLUDE if ANY of these hold:
- The gene is relevant to the patient's clinical presentation (gene-level evidence).
- A plausible molecular mechanism is supported by the evidence.
- The inheritance pattern is compatible with the observed zygosity.
- The reasoning's uncertainties are about MISSING data rather than contrary evidence.

EXCLUDE only if the reasoning identifies a clear affirmative contrary signal:
- Benign / Likely benign classification with no conflicting interpretation, OR
- Very high population frequency consistent with a common polymorphism, OR
- The gene's function is clearly unrelated to the patient's phenotype.

Distinguish carefully: "no evidence found" is a reason to INCLUDE (uncertainty),
while "evidence shows it is benign / common / unrelated" is a reason to EXCLUDE.

Output exactly three lines and nothing else:
Include-case: <strongest reason to include, ≤15 words>
Exclude-case: <strongest reason to exclude, ≤15 words>
Decision: INCLUDE or EXCLUDE

Example output:

Exclude-case: variant-level data limited
Decision: INCLUDE
